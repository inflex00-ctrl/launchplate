Paying for it

Nobody should postpone a tooth because of a bill

Three quarters of the plans we write are paid monthly. Ask at reception — it takes about four minutes and there is no charge for asking.

Yes, on plans between €500 and €7,000 repaid over 12 months, subject to approval by our credit provider. You pay exactly the quoted price, split into twelve. Longer terms are available at a published APR — we will show you the total cost in writing before you sign anything.

Routine cover is usually modest — typically €50 to €60 towards an examination and a scale and polish each year. The bigger benefit is on non-routine work. Give us your policy number when you book and we will check your entitlement and submit the claim for you.

Non-routine dental treatment qualifies for relief at the standard rate. We issue a completed Med 2 form at the end of every course of treatment without you having to ask, and you attach it to your annual return.

Then we stage it. Every written plan we produce is ordered by clinical priority, so you can see what genuinely needs doing this year and what can safely wait. We would far rather do the urgent third of a plan than lose you altogether.

Not sure which of these you need?

That is exactly what the examination is for. Book the €65 assessment, and we will tell you honestly what needs doing, what can wait, and what does not need doing at all.
