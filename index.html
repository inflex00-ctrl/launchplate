Rebrand the whole kit from six variables

Colour, hover, contrast, tinted surface, secondary highlight, corner radius. They sit in one block at the top of one stylesheet, with a matching block for dark mode. Pick a client palette and watch the preview rebrand itself — that is the actual mechanism, not an animation of one.

  --brand-accent:   #b0541f;   /* 5 · secondary accent */

}

№ 06 Licence

The licence,
in plain English.

One payment, one licence, no expiry. The only thing you cannot do is sell the kit itself — everything you build with it is yours and your client's.

Yes — included

  • Use it on unlimited client projects — one purchase covers every client you will ever have.
  • Use it on unlimited projects of your own, commercial or otherwise.
  • Charge your client whatever your work is worth. We take €49 once and go away — no cut, no questions.
  • Change anything — layout, colours, copy, code. Merge two kits, or port it into a CMS.
  • Hand the finished files to the client and walk away. They own their website outright. No attribution, no back-link, no licence key.
  • Keep using it forever. The licence does not expire and does not renew.

No — not included

  • Reselling or redistributing a kit as a template, on its own or inside another product.
  • Listing it on a template marketplace, theme store or bundle, modified or not.
  • Sharing your download outside your own company or team — not as a freebie, not "just for a friend's agency".
  • Building a site-builder, theme generator or subscription product whose main value is these files.

Short version: sell the work, not the kit. Everything a normal freelancer or agency does with a template is covered.

№ 07 Questions

Before you spend the €49.

01What exactly do I get after paying?
A download link, straight after Stripe checkout. Inside is a zip containing the kit: every HTML page, the stylesheet, the scripts, the images and a README. It is the same set of files the live demo runs on — nothing is held back for a "pro" version.
02Can I use this for paid client work?
Yes — that is what it is built for. One purchase covers unlimited client projects, forever. There is no per-site fee, no reporting, no licence key to activate, and nothing stopping you charging your normal rate for the work.
03Can I look at everything before I buy?
Every page of every kit is published as a live demo — no signup, no email gate. Use the kit browser to page through the screenshots, then click through the SaaS Launch Kit, the Law Firm Kit or the Directory Kit exactly as a visitor would.
04Realistically, how long does a rebrand take?
The colour work is minutes — six custom properties at the top of one stylesheet, plus a matching block for dark mode. You can try it on the live demo above. Swapping copy and images is the real work, and every page ships with realistic written content rather than lorem ipsum, so you are editing sentences instead of inventing them. Our own estimate for a full six-page rebrand is 4–11 hours against 24–46 from scratch — an honest estimate, not a survey.
05What if it turns out not to fit the job?
Email us within 30 days and we refund it. No questions, no form to fill in, no explanation required. A €49 template is not worth an argument to either of us.
06Will it work with my stack — WordPress, Webflow, React?
The kits are standard HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so you can ship them as they are, or lift the layouts, type scale and components into whatever you normally build in. Plenty of people buy a kit purely to skip the design phase and rebuild it in their own stack — the licence allows that.
07Do I get future kits and updates?
Buy a single kit and you get every future update to that kit. Buy the complete bundle and you also get every new kit we release, free, in the same download — permanently.
08Is the placeholder copy usable, or is it lorem ipsum?
It is written English, industry by industry — real headlines, real service descriptions, real menu items, plausible attorney bios. It is there so the first client walkthrough looks like a website rather than a wireframe. Every word is meant to be replaced before launch, and the demos say so.
09Single kit or the bundle?
If you know exactly which one you need today, buy that one for €49. If you take on more than two site builds a year, the bundle at €99 is cheaper than buying a second kit later — and it keeps growing.
