Account timeline

Every fact about an account, in the order it happened

Product events, invoices, support tickets, emails, NPS responses and CRM notes converge into one chronological record. Open an account thirty seconds before a call and you already know more than the last person who owned it.

  • Identity resolution that maps users to accounts across domains, SSO and shared inboxes
  • Billing state inline — failed payments, plan changes and overages next to usage
  • Full history from day one — we backfill your events, not just what arrives after signup

Signals

Continuous evaluation, not a nightly job

Most tools recompute health once a day and hope nobody notices. Northwind evaluates every account against every active condition as events stream in, so a signal fires within seconds of becoming true — while the customer is still in the product.

  • 40+ pre-built signals tuned for PLG, seat-based and usage-based models
  • Plain-language builder with an escape hatch to raw SQL on every condition
  • Always explainable — each firing links to the exact events that caused it
  • Backtesting against twelve months of history before you turn a signal on

Segments & scoring

Cohorts that are never one export out of date

Build a segment from usage depth, feature breadth, plan, firmographics and billing state. It stays live: an account joins the second it qualifies and leaves the second it stops. Every segment is also a score, so you can rank instead of just filter.

  • Composite health scores weighting adoption, breadth, sentiment and payment health
  • Cohort comparison to see which onboarding path actually predicts retention
  • Shared definitions so CS, sales and finance stop arguing about what ‘active’ means

Playbooks

Turn a signal into a job somebody actually does

A playbook takes a firing signal and produces an action with the context attached: the Slack message with account numbers, the CRM task assigned to the right owner, the drafted email waiting for a human to approve. Every outcome writes back so you can prove which plays worked.

  • Two-way CRM sync with Salesforce and HubSpot, mapping fields you control
  • Approval steps so nothing automated reaches a customer unreviewed
  • Throttles and quiet hours to keep the noise survivable at scale
  • Attribution reporting on saved ARR and influenced pipeline per play

Warehouse sync

Your warehouse stays the source of truth

Read directly from Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift or Postgres on the schedule you choose, and push scored accounts and signal history straight back out. No vendor lock-in, no shadow data model that drifts from the one your analysts trust.

  • Incremental sync on a cron you set, with full lineage on every field
  • Reverse ETL of scores, segments and signal events back to your tables
  • dbt-friendly — point Northwind at your models rather than raw tables

Warehouse setup guide

warehouse.yml 
source: snowflake
warehouse: ANALYTICS_WH
schedule: "*/15 * * * *"

models:
  - name: dim_accounts
    key: account_id
    maps_to: account
  - name: fct_product_events
    key: event_id
    maps_to: event

# push scores and signals back out
reverse_etl:
  enabled: true
  target_schema: NORTHWIND
