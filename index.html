Plain HTML and CSS

Questions

Everything a buyer
reasonably asks.

Nothing here is a marketing answer. If something is still unclear, the demos are free — click through them first.

What exactly do I get for my money?

A ZIP file. Inside it: every HTML page of the kit, one stylesheet, the small amount of vanilla JavaScript that powers things like the pricing toggle, the icons and images used, a README, and the licence in full. All of it is unminified text you can open in any editor.

Can I use this for paid client work?

Yes, and this is the main reason people buy. The licence covers unlimited personal and commercial projects, including sites you build and invoice a client for. You do not need a separate licence per client or per site. The only thing you cannot do is resell the kit itself as a template.

Do I need React, Tailwind, npm or a build step?

No — that is the entire point. Every kit is hand-written HTML and CSS with a little vanilla JavaScript. There is no package.json, no bundler and nothing to install. Open the folder, edit the text, upload it to any host: Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, or a plain old FTP server.

Can I really see everything before I pay?

Yes. Every page of every kit is published as a live demo on this site — not a video, not a slideshow, the actual pages. Click any preview, click through the navigation, resize the window, toggle the theme. Nothing is gated and there is no signup.

How is it delivered, and do I need an account?

Payment is handled by Stripe. As soon as it clears you land on a download page with your files, and the same permanent link is on your receipt. There is no account to create, no password, and no login to lose.

What if it is not right for me?

Reply to your Stripe receipt within 14 days and you get a full refund. No form, no justification, no questions. Digital goods are easy to refund and arguing about €49 is not worth anyone's afternoon.

Do I get updates, and do they cost extra?

Updates to a kit you bought are free, forever — bug fixes, browser fixes and improvements included. The Complete Bundle goes further: every entirely new kit released in the future is included at no extra cost.

Can I change the colours and fonts?

Easily. Each kit's stylesheet opens with a block of CSS custom properties — background, surface, text, accent, radius and font stack. Change those six or so values and the whole kit is rebranded. Everything downstream inherits from them.

Are there hidden fees, upsells or a subscription?

None. The price you see is the total. There is no pro tier, no license key to renew, no analytics beacon and nothing in the files that phones home. Once the ZIP is on your machine, this shop is irrelevant to you.

Which kit should I buy?

Selling software with plans and docs — the SaaS Launch Kit. Launching an AI tool or agent and need a playground, model comparison or waitlist — the AI Product Kit. Selling your own services or showing client work — the Studio & Portfolio Kit. Torn between two of them, or expecting to build more than one site: the bundle is cheaper than any two kits bought separately.
