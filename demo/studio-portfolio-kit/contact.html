Before you send

Three things
worth knowing.

Briefing a studio is uncomfortable. These are the questions people ask us on the first call, answered in advance.

You will not get a form letter

Every brief is read by a founder and answered by one. If we are not right for it we will say so in the first reply and, where we can, name two studios who are.

A budget range is not a price

We ask because a €12,000 problem and a €90,000 problem need different answers, and it is unfair to design a proposal you were never going to buy.

Nothing you send is shared

Briefs stay inside the studio. We are happy to sign your NDA before you send anything, and we have never named a client who asked us not to.
