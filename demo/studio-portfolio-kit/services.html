Honest bit

When we are the
wrong studio.

Saying this out loud saves everyone a fortnight. If two of these describe your situation, we will tell you on the first call.

A

You need it
in three weeks

Unless a Sprint slot happens to be open, we cannot compress a brand system into a month without producing something you will replace within two years. We will happily recommend two studios who work faster than us and do it well.

B

The decision
needs a committee

Design does not survive eleven opinions with equal weight. If there is no single person who can say yes, the work will regress to the least objectionable option — and you will have paid us to make something safe.

C

You already know
what you want

If the brief is “execute this”, hire a very good freelancer — it will be quicker and cheaper, and the result will be better. We are worth the money when the question is still open.
