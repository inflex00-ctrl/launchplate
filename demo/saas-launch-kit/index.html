How it works

Three steps, one afternoon

You do not need a six-week implementation, a solutions architect, or a new data model. You need your events in one place and a clear definition of trouble.

Connect your data

Drop in the JavaScript snippet, use a server SDK, or point Northwind at your warehouse. Most teams are sending events in under an hour — and yes, we backfill your history.

Describe what matters

Pick from 40 pre-built signals for PLG, usage-based and seat-based models, or write your own conditions. No SQL required, though SQL is there when you want it.

Put it in front of people

Signals land in Slack, your CRM, or an inbox your CSMs already read. Every action writes back, so the next quarter’s model gets sharper on its own.

FAQ

Questions worth asking

Still not sure? Our team answers in under an hour during European and US business days.

Talk to a human

How long does implementation actually take?

Median time from signing up to the first signal firing is four days. If your events already live in a warehouse, it is usually the same afternoon — we read from Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift and Postgres without an ETL vendor in between.

Teams instrumenting from scratch use our JavaScript snippet or a server SDK and are typically live within a week, including historical backfill.

Do we need a data team to run this?

No. Signals are built from a plain-language condition builder that anyone in RevOps or CS can use. Everything is also expressible in SQL, and every signal shows you the query it compiles to — so your data team can audit it rather than maintain it.

How is Northwind different from our product analytics tool?

Analytics tools answer “what happened to this metric?” Northwind answers “which account should someone call today, and why?” We resolve users to accounts, join usage with billing and support state, and deliver the result as an action in the tools your team already has open.

Plenty of our customers keep their analytics tool. The two do different jobs.

What happens to our data?

Your data stays yours. We are SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and offer EU data residency on every plan at no extra charge. We never train shared models on customer data, and you can export or delete everything through the API at any time.

Can we start on a trial without talking to sales?

Yes, and most teams do. The 14-day trial is the full product with no seat limits and no credit card. If you want a hand with instrumentation, our solutions engineers are free during the trial — but nothing is gated behind a call.

What does pricing depend on?

Tracked accounts, not seats. Invite your whole go-to-market team on every plan. Full detail, including the annual discount and the enterprise options, is on the pricing page.
