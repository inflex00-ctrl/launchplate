What to expect

Before you arrive

We email a short medical history form. Filling it in at home saves ten minutes in the waiting room and means the dentist has read it before you sit down.

The examination

Forty minutes. Charting, gum measurements, oral cancer screening and X-rays if they are justified. You will see your own teeth on the screen.

The conversation

What we found, what it means, what your options are including doing nothing, and what each option costs. Questions encouraged.

Your written plan

Printed before you leave, ordered by clinical priority, with a fixed price on every line and a quote valid for 90 days.

Booking policies

  • 24 hours’ notice to change or cancel. Short-notice misses may be charged at half the appointment fee — the chair time cannot be filled.
  • Arrive five minutes early for a first visit so we can check your details without eating into your appointment.
  • No referral needed for any treatment, including specialist endodontics and orthodontics.
  • Bring a list of any medication you take, and your insurance details if you have cover.
  • Under-16s must be accompanied by a parent or guardian for the whole appointment.

Before you book

Questions we are asked every week

Routine examinations are normally available within two weeks at Marlowe Quay and rather sooner in Sandyford. Hygiene appointments book further ahead — typically three to four weeks — so it is worth booking your next one as you leave. Emergencies are a different queue entirely: ring before 10:00 and we see 96% of callers the same day.

Say so in the notes box above, and it will be on the screen before you walk in. Nervous patients get a longer slot, a first appointment where nothing is done except talking and looking, and an agreed hand signal that stops everything immediately. Dr Whelan also runs a sedation list on Wednesdays for people who need more than reassurance. You will not be judged, and you are not unusual — roughly one in six of our patients tells us the same thing.

Ring, email or reply to your reminder text at least 24 hours ahead and there is no charge at all. Inside 24 hours we may charge half the appointment fee, because that chair time cannot be given to somebody else at short notice. We waive it for genuine emergencies without making you explain yourself.
