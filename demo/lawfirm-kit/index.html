Why clients stay

The firm you keep for thirty years

More than half of our work comes from clients we have acted for before, or from the lawyers and accountants who advise them. That is the only marketing metric we take seriously.

01

A partner on every matter

The lawyer who takes your first call is the lawyer who argues your motion. We staff deliberately small teams and we do not learn your business on your invoice.

02

Plain answers, in writing

You will be told what is likely, what is possible and what it will cost, in language you can forward to your board or your family without translation.

03

Fees agreed in advance

Fixed fees for defined work, capped estimates for the rest, and a call before any step that changes the number. No client of ours has been surprised by a bill.

04

Trial-ready from day one

We build the record early. Opponents settle on better terms with a firm that is visibly prepared to try the case, and our clients get the benefit of that posture.
