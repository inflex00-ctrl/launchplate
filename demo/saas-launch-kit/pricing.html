Billing FAQ

The awkward questions, answered

Everything about limits, contracts, procurement and refunds — before you have to ask a salesperson.

Ask us anything

What counts as a tracked account?

Any company record that sent at least one event during the billing month. Trials you have not converted, churned customers, and internal test workspaces are all excluded — you only pay for accounts your team is actually working.

Do you charge per seat?

Never. Every plan includes unlimited users. We think charging per seat quietly punishes you for giving the whole go-to-market team visibility, which is the entire point of the product.

What happens if we go over the account limit?

Nothing breaks and nothing is throttled. We email you at 80% and again at 100% of your limit, then give you 30 days to upgrade or trim. Signals keep firing the whole time.

Can we change plans mid-cycle?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated to the day. Downgrades apply at your next renewal so you keep what you paid for. Both are self-serve in Settings → Billing — no email to sales required.

Is there a discount for startups or non-profits?

Companies with fewer than 20 employees and less than two years of trading get 50% off the first year. Registered non-profits and accredited educational institutions get 40% off permanently. Email billing@northwind.com with a link to your company registration and we will apply it the same day.

What do you need for procurement and security review?

Our SOC 2 Type II report, penetration test summary, DPA, sub-processor list and standard MSA are all available under NDA from the trust centre. On the Scale plan we will also complete your security questionnaire, and we accept redlines on the MSA.

Do you offer annual invoicing and purchase orders?

Yes on annual plans. We support bank transfer, invoice with net-30 terms, and purchase order numbers on the invoice. Card payments are also fine on any plan.

What is your refund policy?

If Northwind is not working for you in the first 30 days of a paid plan, email us and we will refund the full amount, no questions and no exit survey. After that, annual plans can be cancelled at renewal.
