How a tool gets on this list

Three steps, and the first two are ours. Most submissions are answered inside two working days.

  1. Submitted or spotted

    Makers submit their own tools and our editors add what they find in the wild. Either way the entry starts as a draft nobody can see.

  2. Installed and used

    We run the tool against a real project for at least a week. If the onboarding is broken or the free tier is a mirage, that goes in the write-up.

  3. Published and kept current

    The listing goes live with pricing, specs and honest caveats. We re-check every entry quarterly and mark anything that has gone quiet.
