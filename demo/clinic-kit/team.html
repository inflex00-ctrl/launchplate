Our team

Eleven people, and no locums

The clinician who examines you is the clinician who treats you. Between them our four dentists have sixty-one years of practice, and every one of them has been here longer than five.

ImplantsRestorative

Dr Aoife Brennan

Principal Dentist & Clinical Director

BDS (TCD) · MFDS RCSI · Dental Council registration 04182

Aoife opened Brightwater above a bakery on Marlowe Quay in 2009 with one chair, one nurse and a conviction that most people are frightened of the dentist because nobody has ever explained anything to them.

Seventeen years later there are two practices and eleven of us, and she still books forty minutes for a first examination. Her clinical work is now mostly implants and larger restorative cases — the sort of thing patients arrive believing is impossible.

She chairs the practice’s clinical governance meeting, mentors two associates through their MFDS, and is the person the rest of the team ring when a case gets complicated.

Education

  • BDS, Trinity College Dublin, 2004
  • MFDS, Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland, 2008
  • Diploma in Implant Dentistry, RCSI, 2013

Special interests

Dr Niall Okonkwo

Specialist Endodontist

BDS (Manchester) · MSc Endodontology (UCL Eastman) · Dental Council registration 07734

Niall does one thing and does it exceptionally well: he saves teeth other people have written off. He works entirely under a surgical operating microscope, which is why his success rate on retreatments — root canals that have already failed once elsewhere — sits well above the published average.

He came to Brightwater in 2016 after six years in a specialist referral practice in Manchester, and he still takes referrals from eleven other Dublin practices.

He is also, for what it is worth, the calmest person in the building. Patients who arrive convinced this will be the worst afternoon of their lives generally leave slightly puzzled about what the fuss was.

Dr Sofia Marchetti

Specialist Orthodontist

DDS (Bologna) · MClinDent Orthodontics (KCL) · Dental Council registration 09011

Sofia trained in Bologna and then at King’s College London, and joined us in 2018 to build the orthodontic side of the practice from nothing. She now runs aligner and fixed-appliance cases for around ninety patients at a time, roughly half of them adults.

She is straightforward about what orthodontics can and cannot do, and she will tell you if a case is better served by a specialist referral or by simply doing nothing. She digitally previews every case before you commit — you see the projected result and the projected number of months before you have paid anything.

Her Saturday clinic runs on the first Saturday of each month for patients who cannot take weekday time off.

Dr James Whelan

Paediatric & Family Dentist

BDS (UCC) · PgCert Paediatric Dentistry (RCSI) · Dental Council registration 10884

James looks after most of the under-16s across both practices, and a good number of the adults who are just as nervous but less willing to say so.

His first appointment with a child under six involves no instruments at all. They sit in the chair, ride it up and down, count their own teeth in the mirror and go home with a sticker. The dentistry, if any is needed, happens on the second visit, by which point the room is no longer frightening.

He also holds the practice’s sedation certification, and runs the Wednesday list for adults who need oral or IV sedation to get through treatment.

How we work

Small enough that everyone knows your name

A practice of this size can do things a corporate chain cannot. These are the ones patients notice.

  • Every case is discussed. We meet for an hour every Thursday morning to review complex plans as a group. Your treatment has usually had four clinicians look at it.
  • We refer out without ego. Oral surgery, periodontal surgery and complex paediatric cases go to specialists we know personally. We would rather lose the fee than do a mediocre job.
  • Continuity across both sites. One clinical record, one plan, one team — whichever practice suits your week.
  • Ongoing training is paid for. Every clinician gets a training budget and protected study leave. That is why we could bring implants, CEREC and microscopic endodontics in-house.
