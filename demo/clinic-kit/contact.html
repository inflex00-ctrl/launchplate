Dental emergency

Severe pain, facial swelling, a knocked-out tooth or bleeding that will not stop — ring the emergency line now rather than using the form below. Out of hours, registered patients reach the dentist on call. If you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, or swelling that is closing your eye, go to an emergency department instead.

Getting here
  • DART: Grand Canal Dock, a seven-minute walk along the quay.
  • Bus: routes 4, 7, 44 and 47 stop on Pearse Street, two minutes away.
