Our story

A coach house, a hearth, and a stubborn idea about fire.

In 2016 we took on a damp 1890s coach house off Marlowe Street with a collapsed roof and a chimney big enough to stand in. We kept the chimney. Everything else — the oak floor, the zinc bar, the twelve tables — was built around it.

There is no gas line in our kitchen. Every plate that leaves the pass has met the fire in some way: charred, smoked, ember-roasted, or warmed on the stone beside it. It makes us slower than most restaurants and considerably more particular about wood.
