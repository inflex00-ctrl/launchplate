Why patients stay

The bit most practices rush, we slow down

Nearly every new patient tells us the same thing: nobody has ever properly explained what is happening in their mouth. We start there.

  • A consultation that is a conversation. Forty minutes, an intra-oral camera on the screen beside you, and time to ask the question you were embarrassed to ask.
  • Fixed written plans. You leave with a printed plan, staged by priority, with a price against every line. It does not change unless you ask us to change it.
  • Single-visit crowns. Our CEREC scanner and mill turn a two-appointment crown into one two-hour visit.
  • Anxious patients genuinely welcome. Longer slots, stop signals agreed in advance, numbing gel before every injection, and oral or IV sedation if you need it.
  • One record across both practices. Book wherever suits your week — your notes, scans and plan follow you.

Spreading the cost

  • 0% finance over 12 months on any plan above €500, subject to approval. No deposit on treatment under €3,000.
  • Med 2 forms issued automatically. Most non-routine dental work qualifies for 20% tax relief — we complete the form for you.
  • Staged treatment. Where it is clinically safe, we phase larger plans across two tax years.
  • Card, transfer or cash — and no charge for the plan itself. Written quotes are free and last 90 days.
